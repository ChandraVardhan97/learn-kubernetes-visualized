# Community conduct

Be respectful, specific and constructive. Discuss ideas and evidence rather than attacking people. Welcome beginners, distinguish mistakes from intent, and make corrections with clear explanations.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing, and sharing another person’s private information are unacceptable. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful contributions and restrict participation.

When public contribution intake opens, the owner will publish a private reporting channel. Until then, do not put sensitive reports or personal information in public issues.
